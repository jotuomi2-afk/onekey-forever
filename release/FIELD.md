# The play behind each version

What the owner's play logs showed for each version, and whether it was
enough to publish it as a release instead of a beta. The rule is in
docs/RELEASE.md ("From beta to release"). Totals only: the logs stay on
the owner's computer.

## 0.38.1: enough for a release

Read on 26.11.2026. Three sessions carry the stamp: 161 minutes and 113
fights, the druid 92 and the mage 21; one death; no fight with the key
pressed and no cast. `--field` says enough.

Seen working in play: the interrupt key of 0.38.1. Alt+F was pressed in
twelve fights, every one of them against a caster, so the cast warning
shows; Bash landed in eleven, one global cooldown after the bear in most.
The mage's opener and G as before.

The death was read: the player stood still for five minutes in cat form
and Prowl, a level 27 mob attacked, and no key was pressed in the 80
seconds before the death. Away from the keyboard; not the addon's doing.

Nothing the owner reported about 0.38.x is open.

## 0.37.1: stays a beta (0.38.0 follows it)

Read on 26.11.2026. Three druid sessions carry the stamp: 84 minutes and
40 fights; no fight with the key pressed and no cast. Missing: six
minutes, 20 fights, and every fight of a second class (the mage was not
played).

One death, read: Stormwind City, the target not named in the log, cat
form, 3.8 seconds, neither F nor G pressed in the session's only fight.
Not the addon's doing.

The finisher comparison is complete: 21 fights on Rip (70.9 DPS, 15.0 s)
and 27 on the Bite (69.6 DPS, 15.4 s), one level won each. No clear
difference; 0.38.0 makes Rip the plan. Still never pressed in play:
Alt+G (run) and Alt+F as a bear (Bash, no cast in the whole log).

## 0.37.0: stays a beta (the numbers are there, the judgement is not)

Read on 25.11.2026. Three sessions carry the stamp: druid 130 minutes
and 39 fights, mage 35 minutes and 46 fights; one death; no fight with
the key pressed and no cast. `--field` says enough.

Held back by judgement:

- The finisher test is still running (9 fights on Rip, 11 on the Bite,
  20 a side needed). A release should hold one settled plan, not a test
  that takes turns.
- Two changed keys were never pressed in play: Shift+G in a fight (the
  bear, then Swipe) and Alt+G (run, since 0.36.0).

The death was read: a pack of eight around a rare level 31 mob, at level
29, that came while the player was out of form casting a buff; G (potion,
Rejuvenation, Nature's Grasp, the bear) was pressed 33 seconds in. No key
did nothing; not the addon's doing.

Seen working in play: the two finisher plans taking turns and noted in
the log; more Shred openers from Prowl (8 of 26 Prowl pulls, 5 of 21
before the big prompt); the mage's opener and G.

## 0.36.2: stays a beta

Read on 24.11.2026. Two sessions carry the stamp: druid 125 minutes and
52 fights, mage 18 minutes and 14 fights; no death, no fight with the key
pressed and no cast. Missing: six mage fights (14 of 20). Seen working in
play: the mage's two Fireballs before Frostbolt (8 of 13 pulls) and Frost
Nova on G (10 of 14 fights), the druid's quest hints. Not used in play:
the druid's Alt+G (no press).

## 0.35.1: stays a beta

Read on 23.11.2026, five characters' logs.

- No session can be tied to 0.35.1: the version stamp on the session line
  comes with 0.35.2, and by the clock the mage's four sessions ended
  before the mage changes of 0.35.x were installed (every pull opened
  with one Fireball, the old opener).
- The druid's last session (27 minutes, 34 fights, no death, no fight
  with the key pressed and no cast) showed the quest hints of 0.35.0 in
  their new form in the real client. That is the only thing of 0.35.x
  seen in play.
- Not seen in play at all: the mage's opener by distance, the mage's G
  (Frost Nova, Blink, Mana Shield), the quest list window, the changed
  lists of the other classes.
