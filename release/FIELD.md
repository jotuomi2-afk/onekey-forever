# The play behind each version

What the owner's play logs showed for each version, and whether it was
enough to publish it as a release instead of a beta. The rule is in
docs/RELEASE.md ("From beta to release"). Totals only: the logs stay on
the owner's computer.

## 0.36.2: stays a beta

Read on 24.11.2026. Two sessions carry the stamp: druid 125 minutes and
52 fights, mage 18 minutes and 14 fights; no death, no fight with the key
pressed and no cast. Missing: six mage fights (14 of 20). Seen working in
play: the mage's two Fireballs before Frostbolt (8 of 13 pulls) and Frost
Nova on G (10 of 14 fights), the druid's quest hints. Not used in play:
the druid's Alt+G (no press).

## 0.35.1: stays a beta

Read on 23.11.2026, five characters' logs.

- No session can be tied to 0.35.1: the version stamp on the session line
  comes with 0.35.2, and by the clock the mage's four sessions ended
  before the mage changes of 0.35.x were installed (every pull opened
  with one Fireball, the old opener).
- The druid's last session (27 minutes, 34 fights, no death, no fight
  with the key pressed and no cast) showed the quest hints of 0.35.0 in
  their new form in the real client. That is the only thing of 0.35.x
  seen in play.
- Not seen in play at all: the mage's opener by distance, the mage's G
  (Frost Nova, Blink, Mana Shield), the quest list window, the changed
  lists of the other classes.
