## pfQuest data

`OneKeyDruid/QuestData.lua` is generated by `tools/extract_pfquest.py`
straight from pfQuest's own repository (revision named in the file's
header). Only pfQuest's MIT-licensed tables go in: quests, units, objects,
items and their English names. No other quest database is used.
